# FLUXFIRE Scientific Integrity

FLUXFIRE is an independent evidence workspace for microgravity-combustion research. It is not a NASA product, NASA endorsement, operational spacecraft-safety system or fire-risk predictor.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Do not invent measurements, units, findings, source access, test outcomes or award probabilities.
- Do not treat a title, abstract, objective or file listing as proof of a reported experimental finding.
- Do not reconcile conflicting values unless a cited source explicitly does so.
- Do not convert unknown or missing conditions into zero.
- Do not assign fire-risk scores or spacecraft safety predictions.

## Evidence categories

| Category | Use |
|---|---|
| Primary experimental data | Source-linked table cells or raw numeric rows. |
| Investigation metadata | Objectives, design notes and PSI catalog fields. |
| Publication metadata | Title, DOI, authorship or citation information only. |
| Reviewed publication finding | Exact reviewed section/locator with quotation or tightly bounded summary. |
| Derived workflow analysis | Counts, coverage, readiness or chart transforms that remain tied to source data. |
| AI interpretation | Extractive passage selection only; no novel scientific prose is accepted as evidence. |

## Comparison limits

Comparisons must show matched, different and missing conditions for material, geometry, oxygen, pressure, temperature, flow, gravity and measurement method. Numerical comparison is withheld when units, methods or required conditions are unavailable or incompatible.

## AI-answer limits

The AI research tools retrieve indexed passages and require exact source IDs and quotations before showing claims. Altered quotes, unknown passage IDs, title-only evidence and unsupported outputs are rejected. If evidence or model access is unavailable, FLUXFIRE must show an insufficient-evidence state.

## Visualization limits

Charts may plot exact same-unit numeric values only. Approximate values, ranges, ambiguous units and unknowns remain visible in tables or notes but are not plotted as precise data.

## Review obligations

Future evidence additions must document access status, reviewed sections/pages, method, conditions, findings, limitations and source-specific citations before they can influence search, comparison, dashboards or exports.
